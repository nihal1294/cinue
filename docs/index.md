Cinue is a local-first TV show and movie tracker for iPhone and iPad. Keep up with episodes, build lists, and explore your viewing history in a private on-device library.

<div class="actions"><a href="/cinue/installation/">Install and run</a><a href="/cinue/user-guide/">Read the user guide</a><a href="https://github.com/nihal1294/cinue">View source</a></div>

## Made for your collection

- **Follow what is next.** See upcoming and recently aired episodes alongside your watch history.
- **Make it yours.** Organize shows and movies with favorites, watchlists, notes, ratings, and custom lists.
- **Keep a copy.** Export and restore Cinue JSON backups, or import supported SeriesGuide JSON backups.
- **Discover more.** Use your own TMDB credential for discovery and metadata refresh. Your library works locally without one.

Cinue has no account, analytics, telemetry, or backend. Automatic iCloud sync is not active. See the [user guide](/cinue/user-guide/) for privacy and backup details.
